The Teen Titans' Summer Smash event is here in DC: Dark Legion, and I want to break down the most important aspects of the event so that you can feel confident you're min-maxing all the most important aspects of it.

## Tips for Spenders

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Pizza_marble_packs.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

If you plan to spend on the event, my recommendation for light spenders would be grabbing the Titan's Reflex Training Bundle to get the portrait frame, grabbing the Titans' Reflex Pass (whichever tier you're comfortable with, but the more expensive one is the best value), and going after the $4.99 USD pack each day for maximum value. Mid-level spenders should buy up to the $19.99 pack each day. Anything after that is generally whale food for these events.

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Pizza_champion_zone.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

For anyone planning to spend on the event, before you make any purchases, be sure to check out the champion zone from your mobile device. There's an event going on where buying planet coins during the Titans' event will get you extra resources. You can then convert those planet coins to buy any packs you want to get in the Titans' event.

## Reflex Pass

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Titans_Reflex_Pass.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

Make sure you're maximizing the amount of marbles you can earn through completing your daily tasks. These are things you do at reset every day anyway, so all you really need to do is  play the game as normal and you'll end up getting these done easily. These rewards can really add up and help you buy more items out of the shop.

## Pizza Celebration

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Pizza_Celebration.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

This is the standard friend gifting part of the event, and while it will eventually cap out so that you get fewer rewards (I believe it's 200 sent / 200 received), you can get a lot of rewards just by completing Daily Tasks, Anti-Crime Network, and by doing Crisis Response and Defeat the Dark Legion on the World Map. Don't forget to send your gifts daily and make as many friends as possible!

## Cyborg's Assistance

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Cyborgs_Assistance.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

Cyborg's Assistance is another reward track that you'll earn by either spending on packs or by getting soda cans from sending and receiving gifts. Non-spenders should not expect to earn much here, but min-maxers have been known to progress pretty far. You can also eventually earn a once daily special item to help you on the main pinball event.

## Titans' Reflex Training (Pinball!)

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Titans_Reflex_Training.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

This part of the event is self-explanatory. You can pick between 1, 5, and 10 marbles at once. I like to pull it back about 40-60%, which seems to yield the highest amount of vouchers.

## Pizza Corner Shop

<img src="/dcdl/guides/May_2026_Event_Teen_Titans_Summer_Smash/Pizza_Corner_Shop.jpg" alt="" style="display:block;width:100%;height:auto;margin-bottom:1.5rem;" />

The shop in these events always favors those who spend, but there are some value packs for non-spenders and lite spenders. If you really want to min-max, the best thing to grow your account is the Afterburners, which are hard to get F2P otherwise. But the anvils and mythic selector shards are always going to be a quality purchase if you don't have a ton of vouchers.

Spenders should always prioritize the magic eyes, as this is a solid way to grab a few extra. Finally, the top prize this event is going to be the Teen Titans base skin. It's not better than the Superman Fortress skin we had last time, but it's a great defensive tool, with a permanent bonus of 10% Physical Defense, which is going to be great for Amazons champions like Queen Hippolyta. It also grants 10% HP to all champions while it is equipped.
