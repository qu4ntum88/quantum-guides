## How to Read This Guide

Every number below is a **floor** — a bare minimum for a 30-day month, not a best case. Each spend level is written for a specific baseline player, and the assumptions behind each line (battles played, wins vs. losses, how many trucks you realistically catch) are listed alongside it. Change the assumption and the number changes with it.

> [!NOTE]
> Shop offers are written in `anvils/gems` notation. The number on the left is how many **World Anvil Fragments** (anvils) the bundle contains; the number on the right is the **Gem** cost. So `3/180` is 3 anvils for 180 gems.

## Non-Spenders (F2P) and Very Light Spenders

### Who this baseline is for

A competitive F2P or very light spender in a competitive league — top 16, so participating in weekly league duels — who **cannot** break into Master 3v3, and competes but loses in events.

The sky is the limit from this baseline.

### What to actually expect

> [!F2P]
> A reasonable F2P expectation in an active league that competes but often loses should be around **526 anvils a month** — and likely more, because you are going to have days where trucks return more than 1 anvil, and months where `3/180` drops in the Mystery Shop multiple times, pushing that 90/month past 100. I can't recall 30 days passing without seeing it several times.

## Sources Deliberately Left Out

These are real sources of anvils, but they are too unreliable or too roster-dependent to put a number on, so none of them are counted in the floors above.

### The league train

There are other sources of anvils, like a successful ride on the league train, but since you can't guarantee a seat in most cases I left it out.

### Worldline purchases

You can also buy anvils from Worldline, but rosters vary wildly and Worldline production cannot be accurately charted or predicted until every character is 5 red stars.

### Worldline particles

> [!NOTE]
> Not factored in, because people's rosters are so wildly different — but you could probably rely on at least **100 anvils a month** from Worldline particles if you are building a stockpile and not using those on magic eyes.

### The VIP shop

> [!WARNING]
> The VIP shop has not been factored into monthly anvils, since `150` gems each is such an awful deal I can't recommend it for F2P or light spend. Maybe medium, if you are desperate to collect every anvil you can during a featured banner.
